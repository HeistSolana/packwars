      How it works

      Duel

      1. Pick a pack

        Choose any live pack, from $25 Pokémon to $5,000 grails, sports, One Piece and more.

      2. Deposit and wait

        Deposit the pack price in USDC. Wait as long as you like, or cancel for a full refund.

      3. Get matched

        When someone deposits for the same pack, both packs are bought at the same moment.

      4. Rip and win

        Both packs open together. The higher insured value takes both cards, sent to the winner's wallet.

      Free holder packs

      1. Fees fill the pot

        Every trade of the coin pays a creator fee into the fee collector.

      2. Snapshot

        Wallets with 1%+ of supply are in. Bonding curve, pool and dev wallets are out.

      3. Fair draw

        A blockhash produced after the snapshot picks two holders. Anyone can re-run it.

      4. Free rip

        Each gets a free pack. Both open at once, and the higher card takes both.
